# SnackPass
Why use SnackPass?
Skip the long queues — Place your snack order in advance instead of waiting at the counter.
Order faster — Quickly search, filter, and select exactly what you want.
Know your total before ordering — See your complete bill clearly before checkout.
Easy order management — Increase, decrease, or remove items anytime before confirming.
Convenient on mobile — Order comfortably from your phone while moving around campus.
No confusion at pickup — Every confirmed order gets a unique token for quick and accurate collection.
Save time between classes — Spend less time waiting and more time studying, relaxing, or getting to your next class.
Simple and transparent — Your selected items, quantities, fees, and final amount are clearly displayed.
Better campus experience — A smoother ordering process means less crowding and a more organized canteen.
Made for everyday campus life — Whether you want a quick snack between classes or a larger order with friends, SnackPass makes it simple.
